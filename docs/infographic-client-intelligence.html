Setup and user guide · Agent A

Client Intelligence Agent

Type a company and the service you sell. The agent researches the company from live sources, checks every claim it is about to make, and drafts a cited briefing, a proposal and an outreach email.

  • ≈3 minper briefing, including your approval
  • 6research angles, run in parallel
  • ≈15model calls in a typical run
  • $0on Gemini’s free tier

Before you start

What you need

One computer, one free key, and two inputs for each run.

On your computer

  • A Windows PC (built and tested on Windows 11)
  • Python 3.10 or newer (tested on 3.14). No extra packages for this agent.
  • An internet connection, for live sources and the model
  • A free Gemini API key from Google AI Studio
  • OptionalA Gmail account with an App password, to send the email

For each run, you provide

  • The company name. A well-known or listed company works best, because the agent reads its Wikipedia page and website.
  • The service line you sell, in a few words, such as “supply chain and distribution”.
  • OptionalYour own document text, such as an annual report.
  • Your approval of the email. Nothing is sent without it.

One-time setup

Set it up in five steps

About ten minutes. After this, you only start the app.

  1. 1

    Install Python

    Download Python 3.10 or newer from python.org. In the installer, tick Add Python to PATH. Then check it in a terminal:

    python --version
  2. 2

    Open the Agents folder

    Keep the Agents folder anywhere on your PC. Every command below runs inside it.

  3. 3

    Add your Gemini key

    Create a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. Copy .env.example to .env and fill in these two lines:

    GEMINI_API_KEY=your-key-here
AGENT_LLM=gemini

    Add GEMINI_API_KEY_2 and GEMINI_API_KEY_3 from other keys for more daily quota. Never share the .env file.

  4. 4

    Start the app

    Double-click start_demo.bat, or run this command. Keep the window open while you work.

    python server.py

    Open http://localhost:8765 and click Client Intelligence Agent, or go straight to /client-intelligence.

  5. 5

    OptionalConnect Gmail, once

    Click ✉ Connect Gmail at the top of the page. Turn on 2-Step Verification in your Google account, create an App password, paste the 16-letter code and click Save and verify. It is stored encrypted on your PC, so you do not repeat this.

RunYour inputs, the live-research switch and the Run button.

Live agent traceEvery step as it happens. The approval form appears at the top.

OutputThe briefing in four tabs, plus the files to download.

  1. 1

    Open the agent page

    Go to http://localhost:8765/client-intelligence.

  2. 2

    Enter the company and the service line

    Type the prospect’s name and what you sell. Leave the document box empty for a real company.

  3. 3

    Check the two switches

    Keep Live research ticked and Reuse cached model answers unticked for a fresh run. To practise safely, click Load fictional sample document, which turns the web off.

  4. 4

    Click Run agent and watch the trace

    The agent plans, researches, verifies, writes and critiques. A run takes about three minutes. Longer is normal when the critic sends drafts back.

  5. 5

    Approve the email

    When Human approval needed appears, edit the subject and body, then click Approve. Reject keeps the email unsent and unfinalized.

  6. 6

    Read the result

    Open the four tabs. Under any claim, click evidence to see its exact quote and source. The tag shows strong or headline-only.

  7. 7

    Download or send

    Download the files from the Output pane. To email it, open the Email panel, click Preview recipients, then Send and confirm.

Good to know

Time, limits and trust

Time and cost

  • About three minutes, mostly the critic’s review rounds.
  • About 15 model calls per run. A free key allows roughly 20 calls a day per model, so a few runs a day. More keys add more.

What it will not do

  • Search the open web. It uses Wikipedia, Wikidata, the company site and live news.
  • Find a contact address. Send the .eml yourself, or use demo mode.
  • Turn a hypothesis into a fact. Hypotheses are inferences and are worded as questions.

Trust and privacy

  • Every claim shows its quote and source.
  • News items are labelled headline-only.
  • Your text goes to Google’s Gemini API. On the free tier, Google may use prompts to improve its products, so avoid confidential material.

Quick fixes

If something looks wrong

What you seeWhat to do
The page does not openStart the app first with start_demo.bat, then open http://localhost:8765.
The run stops with a quota or rate-limit messageWait a few minutes, or until tomorrow. Adding a second key to .env doubles your daily allowance.
Very few findings for a small companyIts Wikipedia page and site may be thin. Paste your own document text into the box.
The Send button is greyed outConnect Gmail first, and approve the email. An unapproved email never sends.
A run takes more than four minutesThis is normal when the critic rejects several drafts. Let it finish.
